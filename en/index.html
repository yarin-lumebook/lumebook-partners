What a partnership includes

Exactly what is included is set out in a written agreement before you start, and every idea is reviewed on its own merits.

  • Suitability review

    Together we look at whether the idea fits a personalized book, where the child appears with their name and an illustrated likeness.

  • A dedicated book page

    A book page on the site with your name and professional background, worded as we agree together.

  • Compensation tied to sales

    Compensation is tied to sales of the agreed book. The amount and how it is calculated are set in the written agreement.

  • A printed copy

    A printed copy of the book.

  • Joint content and promotion

    We can coordinate content and promotion for the book, in a scope agreed in advance between the parties.

  • Monthly reporting

    A monthly report on sales and compensation.

What each side does in writing, review, production and marketing is defined before work starts, in writing.

From your inquiry to the launch, in six steps.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    A short form about your idea, the subject and the audience.

  2. 02

    Suitability review

    We read the inquiry, and if there may be a fit we continue to a conversation.

  3. 03

    Defining the idea and responsibilities

    We talk about the idea and about what each side does.

  4. 04

    Written agreement

    The terms are set out in a written agreement before work starts.

  5. 05

    Preparing and reviewing the book

    The book is prepared and reviewed together, as defined in the agreement.

  6. 06

    Launch and reporting

    The agreed promotion begins, and you receive reports as set out in the agreement.

There are no fixed timelines here. The pace depends on the idea and on the agreement.

07

Questions and answers

Do I need a finished manuscript?

No. You can get in touch with a written story, an existing book, an early idea or professional knowledge alone. There is no need to attach a manuscript to the form. If you have a text, we will be glad to hear about it later.

Can I apply with professional expertise or an early idea?

Yes. The form is also for people who work with children and parents and have not written a book yet. In the conversation we look at whether the knowledge or idea can become a story that fits a personalized book.

Do I need a social media audience?

No. A large audience is not needed, and the form does not ask for follower numbers. If we reach an agreement, it will set out which content and promotion suit both sides.

Who writes and who approves the content?

This is decided before work starts and written into the agreement. Among other things it defines who writes, who reviews the content professionally and who approves the final wording.

How is compensation determined?

Compensation is tied to sales of the agreed book and is set in the written agreement, including how it is calculated. There is no promise of income or of a number of sales.

What promotion is expected of me?

We can talk about joint content and promotion, in a scope agreed in advance between the parties. Everything is set in the agreement, not in the form.

What happens to the rights in an existing text?

The rights in the text and the permitted uses are set out in the written agreement. Sending the form does not transfer any rights. If the text has already been published or is with a publisher, please say so in your inquiry so we can look at what is possible.

Can I also work with a publisher?

You can mention in your inquiry if you already work with a publisher. What is possible alongside the partnership, and on what terms, is set out in the agreement.

How can the partnership end?

The terms for ending, including the timing and how to give notice, are set in the written agreement before you start. Sending the form does not bind either side, and you can stop at any stage.

How is this different from the ambassador program?

The ambassador program is for content creators who promote existing books with a personal link. This is a partnership around an original book or professional knowledge, with its terms set in a separate agreement. Sending the form is not joining the ambassador program, does not create a personal link and does not bind either side.
